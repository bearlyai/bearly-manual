# Bearly Manual

HTML: [https://bearly.ai/docs/manual/](https://bearly.ai/docs/manual/)

> The complete guide to using Bearly, covering every setting and capability, how features connect, and step-by-step recipes that combine them, for people, AI assistants, and agents.

This manual explains how to do anything in Bearly, from your first chat to workflows that chain research, data, media, agents, and automation. Each capability says when to use it, what it needs, what it produces, and what it combines with, and each recipe says why its steps work together, so you can adapt it to your own work.

New to Bearly? Read [What Bearly is](https://bearly.ai/docs/manual/what-is-bearly.md), then [Tour the workspace](https://bearly.ai/docs/manual/workspace-tour.md).

## For AI assistants and agents

This manual is written to be read by software as well as people. Replace the trailing slash of any page address with `.md` for clean Markdown, start at [/manual.md](https://bearly.ai/docs/manual.md) for the directory, or read [/manual-full.txt](https://bearly.ai/docs/manual-full.txt) for everything in one file. [Guide for AI assistants](https://bearly.ai/docs/manual/for-ai-assistants.md) explains how to use it to help someone, and [API and agents](https://bearly.ai/docs/manual/api-and-agents.md) covers programmatic access.

## Keeping it accurate

The manual is written from the current app and updated as features ship. The shorter [feature guides](https://bearly.ai/docs.md) cover single features, and the [changelog](https://bearly.ai/docs/changelog.md) lists every release. If something in the app looks different from what you read here, the app is right; tell us with **Give Feedback** in the account menu, or at support@bearly.ai.
